About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple note-taking tool designed to help you capture ideas quickly and keep them organized. It is useful for recording reminders, tasks, and ideas before they disappear.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note in the New note input field.
  2. Click the Add Note button to save your note.
  3. Review your notes in the Your notes section.

Features

  • Quick and simple note entry
  • Easy-to-read list of notes
  • Keyboard-friendly navigation
  • Simple and clean interface
